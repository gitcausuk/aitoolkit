Privacy and cookies

The toolkit is designed to minimise the personal information it handles.

Information processed by this site

The main toolkit does not ask for personal information. The AI Designer processes text and files in your browser to create a draft assessment redesign. The prototype does not upload that content to a server. Do not add personal, confidential, restricted or unreleased assessment material.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. It does not store your draft in browser storage. Files you download are created on your device.

Third-party services

The AI Designer requests document-reading code and fonts from third-party content delivery services. Those providers receive standard technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details. The document content remains in your browser. Links to University services, ChatGPT and other external sites open services with their own privacy information and cookie choices.

Email

If you email the site owner, your email service and the University mail service will process the information you provide so the message can be answered.

Questions or problems

Email David Dodick about Oxford AI Toolkit privacy to ask a question or report a problem.

This toolkit is independently maintained practical guidance. It is not an official University policy website.
