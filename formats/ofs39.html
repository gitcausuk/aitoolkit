Aural examination

These are design suggestions, not University policy. Follow your approved assessment instructions.

A series of exercises that assess listening skills, typically in relation to language or musical competency.

Open in the Assessment DesignerCreate an editable draft for local review.

Strengthen evidence of learning

Live or closely invigilated conditions can provide strong evidence of listening and spontaneous response. Use varied stimulus material and appropriate spontaneous tasks for assessment quality. Declare and implement the artificial intelligence (AI) permission clearly, including any authorised assistive technology or reasonable adjustments.

Options involving AI

Retain listening comprehension where that is the intended capability, including agreed reasonable adjustments. Staff use of AI to draft stimuli is item preparation, not student AI use. If critical evaluation of generated audio is an outcome, design and validate it as a separate task with appropriate criteria.
