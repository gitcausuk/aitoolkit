Concise documents used to communicate the outcomes of an experiment or small-scale project.

Strengthen evidence of learning

Tie the report to the relevant experimental or practical evidence and ask students to justify methodology, interpretation and limitations. Raw data, notebooks or supervisor records can provide context but are not proof of authorship. Declare permitted artificial intelligence (AI) use for analysis, visualisation or writing support and specify how outputs must be checked.

Options involving AI

Where appropriate, permit AI support for analysis code or chart drafts, with checks against known results or independent calculations. If independent interpretation is assessed, exclude AI-generated interpretation from that component. AI-assisted interpretation is a different design requiring explicit criteria.
