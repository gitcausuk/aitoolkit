Business plans and design proposals

These are design suggestions, not University policy. Follow your approved assessment instructions.

Documents which set out to persuade potential funders of the feasibility and profitability of a new product or service and how this will be achieved.

Strengthen evidence of learning

Use a defined client brief, evidence base and decision context. Assess feasibility, trade-offs and justification rather than polish alone. An oral pitch, process documentation or client interaction may provide additional evidence where aligned, accessible and approved.

Do not rely on the scenario being unfamiliar to artificial intelligence (AI). Declare permitted AI use and verification responsibilities.

Options involving AI

Students may use AI to propose design alternatives or challenge a business model. They test assumptions against evidence and justify feasibility. Distinguish generated estimates from verified market or financial evidence.

Protect independent ideation if assessed, and specify the permitted AI role and declaration.
