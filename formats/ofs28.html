Fieldwork, site, museum and industrial visit reports

These are design suggestions, not University policy. Follow your approved assessment instructions.

Assess students' learning through fieldwork, a site or museum visit or an industrial visit. Generally presented in a clearly structured format.

Open in the Assessment DesignerCreate an editable draft for local review.

Strengthen evidence of learning

Tie the task to the relevant fieldwork, visit or situated experience. Use dated notes, observations, material evidence or reflection where these genuinely evidence the intended learning, while avoiding unnecessary surveillance. Declare artificial intelligence (AI) permissions for the write-up phase and make clear that contemporaneous records provide context rather than proof of authorship.

Options involving AI

Students may use AI to organise permitted field notes or propose themes, then test those themes against observations. Keep recorded evidence distinct from generated interpretation. Restrict AI where independent observation or analysis is assessed.

Require a supported account of findings and the prescribed declaration.
