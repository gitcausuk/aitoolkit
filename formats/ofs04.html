Strengthen evidence of learning

Controlled, device-restricted and invigilated conditions can provide strong evidence of independent performance when effectively and accessibly implemented. Ask students to show workings, justify method choices and explain why an approach is appropriate. Vary parameters and scenarios for breadth and quality, not as a guarantee against assistance.

Options involving artificial intelligence (AI)

Supply an AI-generated worked solution with a plausible error or questionable assumption. Students diagnose the problem, correct the method and explain the consequences. Retain independent problem-solving tasks where generation of a solution is also assessed.
