Strengthen evidence of learning

Use a specific case, decision context and supplied evidence. Ask for a prioritised recommendation, explicit reasoning, assumptions and trade-offs. Process documentation or an oral presentation may add evidence where it supports the intended learning and is proportionate, accessible and approved.

Declare any permitted artificial intelligence (AI) use and the required declaration.

Options involving AI

Students may use AI to generate competing solutions or stakeholder perspectives, then assess feasibility and defend a prioritised recommendation. Protect independent option-generation if that is an intended capability. Specify the permitted assistance and mark evidence, trade-offs and judgement, with the prescribed declaration.
