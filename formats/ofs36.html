Musical performance

These are design suggestions, not University policy. Follow your approved assessment instructions.

Assesses practical application of understanding and knowledge. Allows students to communicate their creative practice to wider audiences.

Open in the Assessment DesignerCreate an editable draft for local review.

Strengthen evidence of learning

Live performance can provide strong direct evidence of the assessed practical or artistic capability. Use programme choice, interpretation or improvisation where these align with the learning outcome. For pre-recorded work, consider a planned live commentary or defence only where proportionate and accessible.

Declare artificial intelligence (AI) permissions for related composition or arrangement work.

Options involving AI

Where technical and expressive performance are the outcomes, retain direct assessment of that performance. AI-supported composition or arrangement belongs in a separately specified component if relevant, with its own criteria and declaration. Rehearsal support alone is not an AI-enhanced summative task.
