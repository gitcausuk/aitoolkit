Oral examination

These are design suggestions, not University policy. Follow your approved assessment instructions.

A series of exercises that assess verbal skills, typically in relation to language competency.

Open in the Assessment DesignerCreate an editable draft for local review.

Strengthen evidence of learning

Live, controlled oral conditions can provide strong evidence of spontaneous language production. Use varied topics, registers and responsive prompts with consistent criteria. Declare and implement the artificial intelligence (AI) permission clearly.

Where pre-recorded elements are used, a planned live follow-up may be appropriate if spontaneity is part of the intended learning and the component is accessible and approved.

Options involving AI

Retain live spontaneous speaking where that is the intended capability, including agreed reasonable adjustments. AI conversation practice is preparation, not an enhancement of the assessed task. Evaluate AI-mediated communication only through a separately specified outcome and design; do not imply live assistance is permitted.
