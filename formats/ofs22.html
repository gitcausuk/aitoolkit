Strengthen evidence of learning

Clarify whether the poster assesses content, visual communication, oral response or all three. Use a live question-and-answer element only where oral explanation is part of the intended learning and it can be consistently and accessibly implemented. Include original or supplied evidence where relevant and use reflective process evidence proportionately.

Set permissions by the capability being assessed. If prioritisation or visual design is assessed, preserve evidence of those student decisions before AI alternatives are introduced, or restrict AI support accordingly. Students verify content and justify the final communication choices.
