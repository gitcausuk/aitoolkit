Strengthen evidence of learning

Where online invigilation is approved and accessible, use appropriate platform controls and clear contingency arrangements; do not assume they eliminate unauthorised assistance. Randomise item order where suitable and design application-level, scenario-based questions. Declare and implement the artificial intelligence (AI) permission clearly in advance.

Options involving AI

Present an AI-generated rationale in a scenario-based item. Students select its strongest criticism or the evidence that would change its conclusion. Validate items and scoring.

The fixed stimulus does not itself prevent outside AI assistance; retain the declared invigilation and AI-use conditions.
