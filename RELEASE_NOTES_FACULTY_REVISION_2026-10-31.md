# Faculty page revision — 31 October 2026

## What changed
- The Teaching and assessment page now leads with 40 assessment formats, a single prominent PDF action and a secondary plain-text reading link.
- AI-use instructions, University requirements and marking guidance remain directly accessible as three task buttons.
- The Assessment Designer is a secondary guided review, with an immediate sample result before completing the form.
- The Designer's brief is optional reference text; its limitations are explained before starting.
- Both wording tools now share the same drafting rules. Permitted and excluded activities come from the educator rather than fixed suggested lists. Approved local wording can be preserved in either tool.
- Generated student declarations and their copy button have been removed from the Designer. Its outputs direct educators to the declaration format prescribed locally.
- The Playbook remains available as checklists by assessment type, with a visible plain-text alternative. The standalone VIVA link is removed from the teaching page; its file is retained. Relevant principles remain in the review guidance without requiring users to learn the acronym.
- Official policy and CTL guidance are identified separately from Toolkit suggestions.
- The Designer links to the complete 40-format PDF for its current AI-enhanced options; its underlying format data has not been expanded into a second copy of that column.
- Internal Designer navigation uses the same tab. Task panels have a persistent accessibility target and restore focus when closed.
