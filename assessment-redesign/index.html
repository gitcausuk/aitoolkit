Check an assessment for AI

One question about a brief you set: if students can use generative AI, will this assessment still show you what each student has learned, and what is the smallest change that would help?

It takes about 15 minutes and ends with a draft revised brief you can take to review. It does not decide what University policy permits or approve a change. Your department, programme and approval route decide that, and the tool shows where each piece of guidance comes from.

How this works

The tool reads your brief with fixed rules, matches it to a curated library of redesign approaches drawn from the Toolkit's design guidance, and fills in wording templates. It does not call an AI model, and nothing you paste leaves your browser. Ratings are design judgments, not measurements. You make every decision, and nothing here approves an assessment.
