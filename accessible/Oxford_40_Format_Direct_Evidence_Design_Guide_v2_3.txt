        Oxford summative assessment formats
        All 40 formats with proportionate direct-evidence design strategies

PURPOSE                    Use this reference when selecting or strengthening an Oxford summative assessment format. The strategies focus on making the intended knowledge, reasoning, judgement, process or
                           performance visible. No format, process record or oral component automatically proves authorship or eliminates unauthorised assistance. Where AI could materially obscure the capability
                           being assessed, use more than one proportionate source of evidence and ensure any additional component is specified in advance, accessible, consistently applied and locally approved.
